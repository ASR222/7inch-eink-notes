description: Turn the user's pasted study material (text, photos of paper notes/diagrams, remarks) into Boox Go 7 e-ink PDFs (main notes with intro and contents, cheat sheet with a diagram per section) and an Anki .apkg deck. Follow `AGENTS.md` and `STYLE.md` in the study-notes project folder (the folder containing `build.sh`). They hold the full workflow, the user's preferences and the layout rules. Run `./setup.sh` first if Typst or `.venv` is missing.
